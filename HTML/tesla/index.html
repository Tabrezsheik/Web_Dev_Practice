Nicola Tesla

Early Life

Nicola Tesla was a Serbian-American inventor, electrical engineer, mechanical engineer, and futurist who is best known for his contributions to the design of the modern alternating current (AC) electricity supply system.

Tesla was born on July 10, 1856, in Smiljan, Croatia. He was the fourth of five children in a Serbian family. His father was a priest in the Serbian Orthodox Church, and his mother was an inventor of household appliances.

He showed an early interest in electricity and engineering, and he was known for his photographic memory and ability to visualize complex electrical systems in his mind.

He attended the Austrian Polytechnic in Graz, Austria, where he studied electrical engineering. However, he did not complete his degree and later moved to Paris to work for the Continental Edison Company.
